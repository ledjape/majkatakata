About Katerina

Empowering Parents to Trust Their Baby’s Instincts

Hello and welcome! My name is Katerina Galić, and I am a certified instructor for complementary feeding of newborns according to the Baby Led Weaning (BLW) method.

The BLW approach is more than just a feeding method—it is a philosophy that encourages babies to discover food on their own, at their own pace. It respects their independence, instincts, and natural curiosity.

In recent years, pictures of tiny babies holding big pieces of food have flooded social media. Yet in our local community, BLW is still a fresh concept that brings questions, doubts, and worries. My mission is to offer clear, practical, and evidence-based guidance while supporting parents every step of the way.

Addressing Common Parent Concerns

Research demonstrates that babies starting BLW are not at higher risk of choking compared to traditional spoon-fed infants. What matters most is following proper food preparation rules (size, texture, shape) and ensuring your baby sits upright in an un-reclined high chair.

Mess is a vital sensory milestone! Touching, squeezing, and exploring food helps babies feel comfortable with textures. Using splash mats, long-sleeved bibs, and maintaining a relaxed mindset turns mealtime into joyful learning.

Most babies show readiness around 6 months when they can sit unsupported, have good neck control, and demonstrate curiosity about food while reaching out to grasp items.
